Moving around

W A S D or the arrow keys walk. Drag the view to look. Click the floor to walk there. Use Overview for a view from above.

How it works

Looking and walking
Drag to look. W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, hold Shift to go faster. Click the floor to walk there. On a phone use the Forward and Back buttons.
The Hall of Trophies
The main IFWL cabinets line the walls. Click a trophy to read about it, or use the buttons along the bottom to go straight to a cabinet. The big doors at the end lead to your room.
My Room
Tap a trophy in the panel to place it, then drag it onto a shelf, the mantel, a table or inside a cabinet. Click one to select it, Q and E turn it, scroll while dragging also turns it, Delete puts it back in the box.
Furniture
Add cabinets, a dresser, tables and plinths. Drag them around. They snap to walls, and the trophies on them move with them.
