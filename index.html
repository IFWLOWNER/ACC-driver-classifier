The IFWL Rating

One number that shows how you really race.

Not just lap times. Your rating is built from four things that make someone good to race against, and it moves after every event.

  • Integrity. Your stewarding record. Clean racing keeps it high and every licence point costs you.
  • Fair play. What other drivers say about racing with you.
  • Wheelcraft. How cleanly you drive, lap after lap, across ACC and LMU.
  • Laps. Your progression, your finishes and how often you show up.

The Career economy

Race. Earn. Spend it where it counts.

Every finish pays out. Cover your entry, tyres, fuel and parts, take your prize money and decide how to build your season.

  • Real race earnings. Paid by position in your class, automatically after each race.
  • Sponsors. Weekly sponsorship rolls that top up your balance.
  • Driver Backing. Get a backer behind you and share the winnings.
  • Marketplaces. Trade parts and buy kit with the money you earn.

Kit Studio

Design your own helmet and race suit.

Your colours, your number, your sponsors. Build it in the studio and it shows up everywhere: your licence card, the podium and the driver lineup.

  • Full designer. Colours, shapes, text, numbers, visors and pictures, on helmet, suit, gloves and boots.
  • Your own avatar. A full-body render of you, used across the whole site.
  • Trade and collect. List your designs and buy other drivers' kit in the marketplace.
